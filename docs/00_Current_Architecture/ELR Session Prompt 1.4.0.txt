### Architectural Map

The directory:

`docs/01_Architecture/`

is the authoritative architectural map of ELR.

It must be used to orient repository navigation and architectural
cross-checks.

Before evaluating the consistency, dependency or ownership of a document,
the assistant must consult the relevant architectural documents in
`docs/01_Architecture/`.

The architectural map does not replace the specialist specifications.

It defines the architectural structure, relationships and navigation context
within which specialist documents must be interpreted.

When a specialist document appears to conflict with the architectural map,
the conflict must be identified and investigated explicitly rather than
resolved by assumption.
